## Contributing factors

To be fully transparent, and in the interest of complete clarity for all stakeholders involved, this section walks through what we currently know about the March 4 checkout slowdown. Elevated p99 latency on the payments path was associated with the connection-pool change shipped in build 4127, which lowered the per-host pool size from 64 to 16. The retry storm that followed may have extended the incident by roughly 20 minutes, but we did not measure retry volume directly during the window, so that figure is a reconstruction from load-balancer logs. In the three regions we sampled, rolling back the pool change restored p99 to its baseline within 6 minutes; we have not yet tested whether the same holds for the two regions that were drained during the incident. It bears repeating that these are our current findings based on the information available at this time, and that the investigation is ongoing.
