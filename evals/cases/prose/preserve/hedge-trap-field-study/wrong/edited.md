## Results

Mulching with chipped alder increased 120-day survival from 58% to 71% across 412 seedlings in 9 plots at the Harrow Ridge site. The gain comes from reduced frost heave rather than added nutrients; soil temperature and nitrogen were outside the scope of this analysis. On north-facing plots the gain was smaller (95% CI, -4 to 11 percentage points). These results demonstrate that mulching improves establishment on exposed slopes.
