## Results

It is of paramount importance to situate the following findings within the broader landscape of restoration ecology, a field that continues to evolve in exciting and meaningful ways. In this sample of 412 seedlings across 9 plots at the Harrow Ridge site, mulching with chipped alder was associated with higher 120-day survival (71% versus 58% in unmulched plots). The difference may reflect reduced frost heave rather than added nutrients, but we did not measure soil temperature or nitrogen, so the mechanism remains unresolved. On north-facing plots the survival gain was smaller and not statistically distinguishable from zero (95% CI, -4 to 11 percentage points). These results suggest, but do not establish, that mulching could improve establishment on similar exposed slopes. All in all, at the end of the day, these findings shed important light on a complex and multifaceted issue that merits further attention going forward.
