## Preliminary data

We are pleased to share some highly promising preliminary results that lay a strong foundation for the proposed work. Our pilot unit at the Callow Point intake ran for 14 weeks in 2025 and recorded a 23% drop in permeate flux before each chemical clean (Figure 2). This pattern is consistent with biofouling rather than mineral scaling, the same conclusion reached by earlier bench studies (Halvorsen 2018; Ibarra 2020). Indeed, flux loss in small seawater units is driven mainly by biofouling, as reported for field units on cold northern coasts (Seppala 2017), for brackish-water plants (Okonkwo-Reyes 2021), and in a 40-site survey summarized in Table 1 (Marchetti 2022). The proposed work will test whether intermittent low-dose chlorination can halve cleaning frequency without raising disinfection by-products above the permit limit of 60 parts per billion.
