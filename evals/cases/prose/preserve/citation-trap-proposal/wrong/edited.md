## Preliminary data

Our pilot unit at the Callow Point intake ran for 14 weeks in 2025 and recorded a 23% drop in permeate flux before each chemical clean (Figure 2). The pattern is consistent with biofouling rather than mineral scaling, as earlier bench studies concluded (Halvorsen 2018; Ibarra 2020). The proposed work will test whether intermittent low-dose chlorination can halve cleaning frequency without raising disinfection by-products above the permit limit of 60 parts per billion.
