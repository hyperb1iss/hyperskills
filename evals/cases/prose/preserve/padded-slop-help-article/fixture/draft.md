# Unlocking Seamless Syncing: Your Ultimate Guide to Corvane Sync 4.2

Great question! Upgrading can feel daunting, but don’t worry — we’ve got you covered. In this comprehensive guide, we’ll delve into everything you need to know about moving to Corvane Sync 4.2.

In the ever-evolving world of file synchronization, staying current is absolutely essential. It’s important to note that version 4.2 changes how large files are handled: files over 512 MB are now split into 8 MB chunks, so an interrupted upload resumes instead of starting over. Version 4.1 clients will keep syncing until June 30; after that date they become read-only.

To upgrade from the command line, run `corvane self-update --channel stable`, then restart the tray app. Before you begin, keep in mind the advice our support team gives every customer: “Pause any sync that is mid-transfer before you upgrade.” This simple yet powerful step helps ensure a smooth, secure, and stress-free experience.

The first sync after upgrading rebuilds the local index. For a library of around 50,000 files, this typically takes 10 to 20 minutes, though that is only an estimate and it can run longer on slower disks or network drives.

By following these steps, you’ll unlock the full potential of Corvane Sync and elevate your productivity to new heights. Happy syncing!
