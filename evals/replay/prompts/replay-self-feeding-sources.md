Synthesis planning, which the handbook distiller uses, picks its sources from search results and from the neighborhood of explicitly requested sources. Nothing stops it from selecting records that are themselves synthesis or reflection output, so a handbook can cite a prior handbook and compound its own summarization loss.

Searched and neighborhood sources must exclude any record whose metadata `capture_surface` is `synthesis_artifact`, `reflection`, `reflection_candidate`, or `reflection_source`, or whose `capture_mode` is `synthesis`. Sources the caller names explicitly stay eligible.
